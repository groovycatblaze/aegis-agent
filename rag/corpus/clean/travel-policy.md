# Domestic Travel Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers business travel within India. Travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk where possible.

## 2. City tiers
Tier 1 cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Gurugram and Noida.
Tier 2 cities: Jaipur, Kochi, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Indore, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Bhubaneswar, Visakhapatnam and Goa.
All other cities are Tier 3.

## 3. Hotel limits per night
Tier 1: up to INR 8,000 per night. Tier 2: up to INR 5,500 per night. Tier 3: up to INR 4,000 per night.
Amounts above the cap are not reimbursed.

## 4. Meal per diem
Meals are reimbursed as a daily per diem, without receipts, for each day of travel (nights plus one).
Tier 1: INR 3,000 per day. Tier 2: INR 2,200 per day. Tier 3: INR 1,600 per day.

## 5. Airfare and local transport
Economy class airfare is reimbursed at actual cost. Local transport such as taxis and cabs is reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day.

## 6. Submitting a travel reimbursement
Submit one claim per trip with category travel. Trips whose reimbursable total exceeds INR 50,000 need manager approval, in line with the Expense and Reimbursement Policy.
