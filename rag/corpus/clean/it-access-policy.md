# Software and Systems Access Policy

## 1. Principle of least privilege
Access to software, SaaS applications and internal systems is granted on the principle of least privilege: employees receive only the access they need to do their job.

## 2. Requesting access
To request access to an application such as GitHub, Jira, Salesforce, Figma, Tableau, AWS or the VPN, raise an IT service desk ticket with category access_request.
The ticket must state the application, the level of access required and the business reason.
The employee's manager is notified of every access request so that they can confirm the business need.

## 3. Standard turnaround
Standard application access is provisioned within 2 business days once the ticket is raised.
Administrator or production access requires an additional review by IT Security and can take up to 5 business days.

## 4. Access reviews
Managers review their team's access every quarter. Access that is no longer required is revoked.

## 5. Departures
All access is revoked on an employee's last working day.
