# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General principles
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses supported by itemised receipts. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense.

## 2. Approval thresholds
Any single claim above INR 50,000 requires approval from the employee's manager before submission.
Any single claim above INR 2,00,000 additionally requires approval from Finance.
Employees can never approve their own claims.

## 3. Meals and entertainment limits
Working meals: up to INR 1,500 per person.
Team events and team lunches: up to INR 2,500 per person.
Client entertainment: up to INR 5,000 per person, including the client guests; record the client's name.
Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of client entertainment.

## 4. Other categories
Training and certifications: up to INR 60,000 per year with manager agreement.
Home-office equipment: up to INR 15,000 per year.
Home internet: up to INR 1,500 per month for hybrid employees.

## 5. Non-reimbursable items
Fines, personal travel, upgrades to premium cabins and minibar charges are not reimbursable.
