# IT Hardware Replacement Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines when employees of Veridian Labs are eligible for a replacement laptop and how replacement requests are raised, prioritised and approved.

## 2. Eligibility for laptop replacement
Employees may request a replacement laptop once their current laptop is 36 months old or older, measured from the purchase date recorded in the asset register.
A laptop younger than 36 months is replaced only when it has a verified hardware failure that cannot be repaired economically, for example a failed motherboard, a swollen battery, a laptop that will not boot, or physical damage.
Performance complaints such as a laptop being slow are not by themselves grounds for early replacement; IT will first attempt an upgrade or a clean re-image.

## 3. Hardware tiers and cost
Standard tier: Latitude 7450 with 16GB RAM and 512GB SSD, estimated cost INR 68,000. This is the default for all departments.
Performance tier: ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 with 32GB RAM and 1TB SSD, estimated cost INR 92,000. This tier is issued to employees in the Engineering department.

## 4. Approval
Hardware requests with an estimated cost above INR 75,000 require approval from the employee's manager before the order is placed.
Requests at or below INR 75,000 are approved automatically by the IT service desk once eligibility is confirmed.

## 5. How to request a replacement
Raise an IT service desk ticket with category hardware_replacement. Include the asset ID, the age of the current laptop, a description of the problem and the estimated cost for the applicable tier.
The employee is notified when the ticket is created and again when the new device is ready for collection.

## 6. Priority
A laptop that has failed completely and leaves the employee unable to work is priority high. Swollen batteries are a safety hazard and are priority critical; stop using the device immediately.
Planned refreshes of laptops older than 36 months that still work are priority medium.

## 7. Return of old equipment
The old laptop must be returned to IT within 10 business days of receiving the replacement so that it can be securely wiped.
