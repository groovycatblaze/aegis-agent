# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## 1. Hybrid schedule
Employees on hybrid contracts work from the office at least 3 days per week and may work from home up to 2 days per week.

## 2. Fully remote roles
Fully remote work is available only for roles designated as remote by HR and requires approval from the department head.

## 3. Home-office setup
Hybrid employees can claim a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 10,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, in addition to the annual equipment limit in the expense policy.

## 4. Working abroad
Working from another country for more than 14 days in a year requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax implications.

## 5. Security when working remotely
Always connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office.
