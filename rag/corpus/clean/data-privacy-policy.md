# Employee Data Privacy Policy

## 1. What we hold
HR records include contact details, compensation, performance information and bank details. This data is Restricted.

## 2. Who can access employee records
Employees can view their own complete record. Managers can view the work profile of their direct reports but not their compensation or personal contact details.
Only the HR team can access compensation data and full employee records for other employees. Bulk exports are restricted to HR.

## 3. Sharing
Employee personal data must never be shared outside the company, and never with personal e-mail addresses.

## 4. Requests
Employees can request a copy of their data or a correction by writing to hr@veridian.example.
