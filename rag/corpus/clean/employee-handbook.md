# Employee Handbook

## 1. About Veridian Labs
Veridian Labs builds analytics software for retailers. We have offices in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune and Chennai.

## 2. Working hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Standard working time is 40 hours per week.

## 3. Code of conduct
Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated and can be reported to HR or the ethics hotline.

## 4. Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months, during which the notice period is 15 days. After confirmation the notice period is 60 days.

## 5. Performance reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October.

## 6. Contact points
HR questions: hr@veridian.example. IT help: raise a service desk ticket. Payroll questions: payroll@veridian.example.
