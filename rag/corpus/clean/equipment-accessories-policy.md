# Peripherals and Accessories Policy

## 1. Standard peripherals
Employees can request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset from IT stock by raising a ticket with category hardware_request.

## 2. Docking stations
USB-C docking stations are provided to employees who work at a desk in the office at least 3 days per week.

## 3. Faulty accessories
Faulty accessories are replaced from IT stock. Raise a hardware_repair ticket describing the fault.
