# IT Service Desk SLA and Priorities

## 1. Priority levels
Critical (P1): safety hazards or outages affecting many people. Response within 30 minutes.
High (P2): a single employee is completely unable to work, for example an account lockout or a failed laptop. Response within 2 hours.
Medium (P3): degraded service with a workaround. Response within 1 business day.
Low (P4): questions and minor requests. Response within 3 business days.

## 2. Account lockouts and password resets
If you are locked out of your account or need a password reset, raise a ticket with category account and priority high. The service desk verifies your identity by calling the phone number on your HR record before resetting credentials.
Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone, including IT staff or AI assistants.

## 3. Ticket updates
Requesters can add comments to their own tickets at any time. Only the IT service desk changes a ticket's status to resolved; requesters may close their own ticket if the issue is fixed.

## 4. Ticket status values
Tickets move through the statuses open, in_progress, resolved and closed.
